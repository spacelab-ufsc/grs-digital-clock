\chapter{Work Plan} \label{ch:workplan}

The work plan is divided into four sequential phases. Hardware and firmware teams work in parallel during Phases~3 and~4. Phases~1 and~2 involve both teams together to ensure shared understanding of the system before splitting into parallel tracks.

Table~\ref{tab:workplan} lists all tasks with their phase, assigned team, expected deliverable, and detailed instructions.

    \multirow{3}{3.2cm}{\textbf{Phase 1 -- Project Kick-off}}
        & \cellcolor{colLow}\textcolor{white}{\textbf{\small Both}}
        & Repository setup on GitHub
        & Repository with folder structure, \texttt{README}, \texttt{LICENSE}
        & Inspect existing lab repositories to replicate folder structure. \\[2pt]

    & \cellcolor{colLow}\textcolor{white}{\textbf{\small Both}}
        & Requirements review and baseline
        & Signed-off requirements document
        & Each team member reads all requirements. Open GitHub Issues for any requirement deemed unclear or infeasible. \\[2pt]

    \rowcolor{rowalt}
    & \cellcolor{colLow}\textcolor{white}{\textbf{\small Both}}
        & Development environment setup
        & KiCad + toolchain installed on all machines
        & HW team installs KiCad~8 and clones the repo. FW team installs the chosen SDK (e.g.\ ESP-IDF or STM32CubeIDE) and sets up a build pipeline (GitHub Actions). \\[2pt]

    \midrule

    % ── PHASE 2 ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
    \multirow{3}{3.2cm}{\textbf{Phase 2 -- Architecture}}
        & \cellcolor{colLow}\textcolor{white}{\textbf{\small Both}}
        & System block diagram
        & Block diagram committed to \texttt{/docs/architecture/}
        & One session with all four members. Define major blocks: Power supply, MCU, RTC, Ethernet PHY, RS-485 transceiver, LED driver, 6-digit display, temperature sensor, backup battery. \\[2pt]

    & \cellcolor{colAnalysis}\textcolor{white}{\textbf{\small HW}}
        & Component selection and BOM draft
        & BOM draft in \texttt{/hw/bom/}
        & Select MCU, Ethernet PHY or built-in MAC, RS-485 transceiver, RTC IC,
        LED 7-segment modules, temperature sensor. Search in other lab projects
        for component recommendation. Verify availability and lead time. \\[2pt]

    \rowcolor{rowalt}
    & \cellcolor{colTesting}\textcolor{white}{\textbf{\small FW}}
        & Firmware architecture document
        & FW architecture doc in \texttt{/docs/firmware/}
        & Define RTOS tasks (or super-loop): NTP sync task, RTC management, display refresh ISR/task, RS-485 slave handler, temperature read, brightness control. \\[2pt]

    \midrule

    % ── PHASE 3 ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
    \multirow{4}{3.2cm}{\textbf{Phase 3 -- Design}}
        & \cellcolor{colAnalysis}\textcolor{white}{\textbf{\small HW}}
        & Schematic design in KiCad
        & Schematic committed and passing ERC
        & Divide schematic into sheets: Power, MCU core, Interfaces, Display \& Drivers. Peer-review via pull request before PCB layout starts. \\[2pt]

    & \cellcolor{colAnalysis}\textcolor{white}{\textbf{\small HW}}
        & PCB layout in KiCad
        & PCB layout passing DRC; Gerbers exported
        & Follow IPC-2221 clearance rules. Separate digital and power planes. Place RS-485 and Ethernet connectors at rear panel. Route display connector to front. \\[2pt]

    \rowcolor{rowalt}
    & \cellcolor{colTesting}\textcolor{white}{\textbf{\small FW}}
        & Firmware skeleton and peripheral drivers
        & Compiling FW skeleton with GPIO, UART, I\textsuperscript{2}C, SPI drivers
        & Implement and unit-test each driver on an evaluation board before the
        custom PCB arrives. Commit tests to \texttt{/.../tests/}. \\[2pt]

    & \cellcolor{colTesting}\textcolor{white}{\textbf{\small FW}}
        & NTP client and RTC integration
        & NTP sync demonstrated on eval board
        & Implement NTPv4 over UDP. Validate UTC offset and DST handling for the \texttt{America/Sao\_Paulo} timezone (UTC$-$3 / UTC$-$2 during DST). \\[2pt]

    \midrule

    % ── PHASE 4 ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
    \multirow{4}{3.2cm}{\textbf{Phase 4 -- Integration \& Test}}
        & \cellcolor{colAnalysis}\textcolor{white}{\textbf{\small HW}}
        & PCB fabrication and assembly
        & Assembled and powered-on PCB
        & Send Gerbers to fab. Assemble SMD components. Perform visual inspection before first power-on. Check all voltage rails with a multimeter before connecting the display. \\[2pt]

    \rowcolor{rowalt}
    & \cellcolor{colLow}\textcolor{white}{\textbf{\small Both}}
        & Hardware--firmware integration
        & Clock displaying time on full hardware
        & Flash initial firmware. Verify display, NTP sync, temperature alternation, RS-485 loopback, and backup-battery retention. Log all defects as GitHub Issues. \\[2pt]

    & \cellcolor{colLow}\textcolor{white}{\textbf{\small Both}}
        & Enclosure fabrication and bracket installation
        & Clock installed on wall / bracket in ground station
        & Machine or order enclosure panels. Install PCB with stand-offs. Mount on SUP-M-50-compatible bracket. Verify tilt adjustment and cable management. \\[2pt]

    \rowcolor{rowalt}
    & \cellcolor{colLow}\textcolor{white}{\textbf{\small Both}}
        & Requirements Verification Matrix (RVM) completion
        & Completed RVM committed to \texttt{/docs/}
        & Trace each requirement to its verification evidence (photos, test logs, measurements). Close all open GitHub Issues before final acceptance. \\[2pt]
